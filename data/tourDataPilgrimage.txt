Delhi To Char Dham Yatra 2026
Overview of Char Dham Yatra By Car Package
The Char Dham-Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath-is considered one of the most sacred pilgrimages in India. Nestled in the Himalayas, these holy shrines are surrounded by stunning valleys, rivers, and snow-capped peaks. Our Char Dham taxi services from Delhi and Haridwar make your journey hassle-free, allowing you to focus entirely on the spiritual experience.

Departure Points: Delhi, Haridwar, Rishikesh
Car Options: AC cars, SUVs, Tempo Travellers
Services Included: Door-to-door pickup, professional driver, guidance for pilgrimage
Char Dham Yatra Tour Itinerary (12 Days / 11 Nights)
Day 1 - Delhi to Haridwar (205 Kms / 6 Hrs)
Pickup from Delhi and drive to Haridwar
Evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri and visit Mansa Devi Temple
Overnight stay in Haridwar
Day 2 - Haridwar to Barkot (215 Kms / 7 Hrs)
Drive via Dehradun and Mussoorie, visiting Kempty Falls
Check-in at Barkot hotel and relax overnight
Day 3 - Barkot to Yamunotri (42 Kms Drive + 6 Kms Trek)
Drive to Janki Chatti; trek to Yamunotri Temple
Take a holy dip at Garam Kund, perform rituals, trek back
Overnight stay at Barkot
Day 4 - Barkot to Uttarkashi (82 Kms / 4 Hrs)
En route visit Prakateshwar Cave
Evening visit Vishwanath Temple
Overnight stay in Uttarkashi
Day 5 - Uttarkashi to Gangotri (100 Kms Each Way)
Take holy dip in Bhagirathi River at Gangotri
Perform rituals and enjoy serene Himalayan views
Return to Uttarkashi for overnight stay
Day 6 - Uttarkashi to Guptkashi (223 Kms / 9-10 Hrs)
Drive via Tehri Dam
Evening visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple and Ardh Narishwar Temple
Overnight stay at Guptkashi
Day 7 - Guptkashi to Kedarnath (32 Kms Drive + 14 Kms Trek)
Drive to Gaurikund, trek to Kedarnath Temple
Darshan at Kedarnath and visit Adi Shankaracharya Samadhi
Overnight stay in Kedarnath
Day 8 - Kedarnath to Pipalkoti (14 Kms Trek + 145 Kms Drive)
Morning darshan at Kedarnath
Drive to Pipalkoti for overnight stay
Day 9 - Pipalkoti to Badrinath (72 Kms)
Drive via Joshimath, visit Narsingh Temple, Mana Village, Bhim Pul
Darshan at Badrinath Temple
Overnight stay at Badrinath
Day 10 - Badrinath to Rudraprayag (155 Kms)
Morning darshan and drive to Rudraprayag
Check-in and overnight stay at hotel
Day 11 - Rudraprayag to Rishikesh (135 Kms / 7 Hrs)
Visit Devprayag (confluence of Bhagirathi & Alaknanda)
Check-in at Rishikesh hotel, overnight stay
Day 12 - Rishikesh to Delhi (234 Kms)
Drive back to Delhi and drop at airport/railway station
Tour concludes with memorable experiences
Highlights of Char Dham Yatra
Haridwar: Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri, Mansa Devi Temple
Yamunotri: Shree Yamunotri Ji Temple, Garam Kund, Divya Shila
Gangotri: Holy river dip at Bhagirathi origin
Kedarnath: Shri Kedarnath Ji Temple, Adi Shankaracharya Samadhi
Badrinath: Shri Badrinath Ji Temple, Tapt Kund, Mana Village, Bhim Pul
Scenic Views: Himalayas, rivers, waterfalls, lush valleys

Delhi to Amritsar Golden Temple Tour
Duration : 03 Nights / 04 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Chandigarh - Amritsar

Day 01 : Delhi - Chandigarh (250 Kms / 05- 06 Hrs)
After an early breakfast, drive to Chandigarh. On arrival, check-in at hotel. In the afternoon, visit Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, Rose Garden, and Mansa Devi Temple. Overnight stay at hotel in Chandigarh.

Day 02 : Chandigarh - Amritsar (240 Kms / 04-05 Hrs)
Morning after breakfast, drive to Amritsar through the green fields of Punjab. On arrival, check-in at hotel. In the afternoon, visit Wagah Border to witness the Indo-Pak Border closing ceremony and explore Amritsar city. Overnight stay at hotel in Amritsar.

Day 03 : Amritsar
After breakfast, visit the Golden Temple - the holiest shrine of Sikhism. Later, enjoy shopping in the local markets. Overnight stay at hotel in Amritsar.

Day 04 : Amritsar - Delhi (450 Kms / 08-09 Hrs)
Morning after breakfast, drive back to Delhi. On arrival, transfer to airport/railway station for onward journey.

Delhi To Agra Jaipur Ajmer Pushkar Tour
Duration : 08 Nights / 09 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Jaipur - Pushkar - Ajmer - Pushkar- Agra - Delhi

Day 01 : Arrival Delhi
Arrive in Delhi and transfer to hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 02 : Delhi Sightseeing
Morning after breakfast, proceed for sightseeing of Delhi visiting Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Dargah of Hazrat Nizamuddin Auliya, and Humayun's Tomb. Also visit India Gate and Qutub Minar. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 03 : Delhi - Jaipur (265 Kms / 05 Hrs)
After breakfast, drive to Jaipur and check-in at hotel. Take some rest and later visit Jaipur Bazar. Overnight stay at hotel in Jaipur.

Day 04 : Jaipur Sightseeing
Morning after breakfast, enjoy an excursion to Amber Fort, where the ascent to the fort is on elephant back. Later visit City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jal Mahal (Water Palace), and Jantar Mantar. After lunch, explore Jaipur Bazar. Overnight stay at hotel in Jaipur.

Day 05 : Jaipur - Pushkar (150 Kms / 03 Hrs)
After breakfast, drive to Pushkar and check-in at hotel. Later visit Ajmer Sharif Dargah. Overnight stay at hotel in Pushkar.

Day 06 : Pushkar
After breakfast, spend the whole day at the Pushkar Fair. Enjoy walking through the traditional decorated fairgrounds and witness various activities such as folk dances, camel trading, and races. Overnight stay at hotel in Pushkar.

Day 07 : Pushkar - Agra (350 Kms / 06 Hrs)
After breakfast, drive to Agra and check-in at hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Agra.

Day 08 : Agra - Delhi (210 Kms / 04 Hrs)
Early morning visit the Taj Mahal at sunrise for the best view. Return to hotel for breakfast and freshen up. Later, visit Agra Fort and then drive back to Delhi. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 09 : Delhi Departure
After breakfast, transfer to the airport for onward journey.

(Tour ends with sweet memories)

Delhi to Haridwar Rishikesh Tour
Duration : 05 Nights / 06 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Haridwar - Rishikesh - Mussorie - Delhi

Day 01 : Arrival Delhi & Sightseeing
On arrival, check in at the hotel. Later proceed for a Delhi sightseeing tour which includes Old Delhi attractions like Jama Masjid, Red Fort, Chandni Chowk and Mahatma Gandhi Memorial. In New Delhi visit India Gate, President's House, Qutab Minar, Parliament House, Humayun's Tomb and more. Evening return to hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 02 : Delhi - Haridwar (220 Kms / 05 Hrs)
After breakfast, drive to Haridwar (approx. 5 hrs). On arrival, check in at the hotel. In the evening visit Ganga Ghat and witness the holy rituals. Overnight stay at hotel in Haridwar.

Day 03 : Haridwar - Rishikesh Sightseeing
After breakfast, explore Haridwar visiting Mansa Devi Temple, Pareshwar Mahadev Temple, Bhimgoda Tank, Pawan Dham Temple, Lal Mata Temple, Parmarth Ashram, Bharat Mata Temple, Saptrishi Ashram etc. Later in the afternoon, drive to Rishikesh (approx. 1 hr). On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Rishikesh.

Day 04 : Rishikesh Sightseeing
After breakfast, enjoy sightseeing in Rishikesh - a serene destination known for yoga, meditation and Hindu spiritual learning. Evening free for leisure. Overnight stay at hotel in Rishikesh.

Day 05 : Rishikesh - Mussoorie (350 Kms / 06 Hrs)
Morning after breakfast, drive to Mussoorie. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Mussoorie.

Day 06 : Mussoorie Sightseeing
After breakfast, visit Kempty Falls. Later enjoy sightseeing in Mussoorie including Camel's Back Road, Tibetan Refugee Centre, small Tibetan market, Landour Bazaar, Children's Lodge (Lal Tibba) - the highest point in Mussoorie, and Sister's Bazaar. Overnight stay at hotel in Mussoorie.

Day 07 : Mussoorie - Delhi
After breakfast, drive back to Delhi. On arrival, transfer to airport / railway station for onward journey.

(Tour ends with sweet memories)
