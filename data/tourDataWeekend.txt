Delhi To Jim Corbett Wildlife Tour Car
Duration : 02 Nights / 03 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Jim Corbett - Delhi

Day 01 : Delhi - Corbett
Depart from Delhi late in the evening by coach for Corbett.

Corbett National Park: India's oldest and one of the finest national parks, spread over 1318 sq. km in the Himalayan foothills. The park is famous for its open grasslands, dense forests, and the Ramganga River flowing through its length. Named after the legendary hunter-naturalist Jim Corbett, the park is also India's first and largest Tiger Reserve sponsored by the World Wildlife Fund. It is home to more than 50 species of mammals, 580 species of birds, and 25 reptile species.

Overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 02 : Corbett
Arrive at Corbett and check into the hotel. After breakfast, morning at leisure to relax and enjoy resort facilities. After lunch, proceed for an adventurous jeep safari into the park for wildlife viewing and to spot the majestic tigers.

Corbett has the highest density of tigers in India (approximately one every 10 sq. km) and was the launch site of the prestigious Project Tiger in 1973. The park is also home to four species of deer - hog deer, sambar, chital, and barking deer - along with wild boars, elephants, Himalayan black bears, sloth bears, wild dogs (dhole), and several smaller cats. Bird lovers can enjoy spotting over 580 species of colorful birds including pheasants, jungle fowl, hornbills, and water birds.

The Ramganga River shelters gharials, marsh crocodiles, and is rich in fish like the prized mahseer, goonch, and brown trout (though angling is not permitted within the park). Rest of the day at leisure for independent activities and resort relaxation.

Overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 03 : Corbett - Delhi
Early morning jeep safari into the park for wildlife viewing. Return to the resort for breakfast. Morning at leisure, followed by lunch. Later, check out and depart for Delhi by coach.

Delhi To Jaipur Weekend Tour
Duration : 02 Nights / 03 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Jaipur - Delhi

Day 01 : Delhi - Jaipur
Drive from Delhi to Jaipur, popularly known as the 'Pink City'. Jaipur is a bustling capital city and business hub, modern yet deeply rooted in its age-old traditions. The old city, painted in pink, captivates every visitor with admiration. The stunning forts of Nahargarh, Amer, Jaigarh, and Moti Dungri stand as dramatic reminders of the city's glorious past and lingering romance. Overnight stay at the hotel in Jaipur.

Day 02 : Jaipur Sightseeing
After breakfast, proceed for a sightseeing tour of Jaipur, covering:

The City Palace: A former royal residence built in a mix of Rajasthani and Mughal styles, featuring an art gallery with miniature paintings, carpets, royal paraphernalia, and rare astronomical works in Arabic, Persian, Latin, and Sanskrit.
Hawa Mahal: Built in 1799, the Palace of Winds is a five-storey pink sandstone building with semi-octagonal and delicately carved windows.
Jantar Mantar: The largest of Maharaja Jai Singh's observatories, with scientifically designed instruments representing the height of medieval Indian astronomy.
Amer Fort & Shila Mata Temple: A magnificent complex of palaces, halls, gardens, and temples built by Raja Man Singh. Visitors often enjoy an elephant ride to the fort entrance. The revered Shila Mata Temple inside the fort houses the goddess idol brought from Jessore (now Bangladesh).
Overnight stay at the hotel in Jaipur.

Day 03 : Jaipur - Delhi
Morning free for leisure or independent activities. Later, check out from the hotel and drive back to Delhi. Tour concludes with fond memories.

Delhi To Mussoorie Weekend Tour Packages

Duration : 02 Nights / 03 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Mussoorie - Delhi

Day 01 : Delhi - Mussoorie
Car hire in Delhi Drive to the fascinating hill resort of Mussoorie, known for its green hills, varied flora and fauna, and fairyland atmosphere. The town offers mesmerizing views of the extensive Himalayan snow ranges to the northeast and glittering views of the Doon Valley, Roorkee, Saharanpur, and Haridwar to the south. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Mussoorie.

Day 02 : Mussoorie Sightseeing
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day sightseeing tour of Mussoorie:

Camel's Back Road: Famous for its picturesque sunset views and the natural rock formation resembling a sitting camel. Ideal for walks and horse rides.
Gun Hill: The second-highest peak of Mussoorie (2,122 mts), accessible by a thrilling ropeway ride. Offers panoramic Himalayan views.
Mussoorie Lake: A newly developed picnic spot with pedal boating and enchanting views of the Doon Valley.
Vinog Mountain Quail Sanctuary: Established in 1993, spread over 339 hectares, famous as the habitat of the now-extinct Mountain Quail (last spotted in 1876).
Kempty Fall: The most popular and biggest waterfall of Mussoorie, located 15 km away in a beautiful valley.
Bhadraj Temple: Dedicated to Lord Bal Bhadra, this spot (15 km away) is ideal for trekking via Park Toll-Clods End.
Municipal Garden: A perfect picnic spot with an artificial lake and boating facilities.
Childer's Lodge (Lal Tibba): The highest peak of Mussoorie, about 5 km away, offering exhilarating snow views.
Jharipani Fall: Located 8.5 km away on Mussoorie-Jharipani Road, accessible partly by car and partly on foot.
Bhatta Fall: Just 7 km from Mussoorie, this scenic waterfall is ideal for picnics and bathing.
Nag Devta Temple: An ancient temple located 6 km away, offering charming views of Doon Valley and Mussoorie.
Return to the hotel in the evening. Overnight stay at hotel in Mussoorie.

Day 03 : Mussoorie - Delhi
Morning free for leisure activities. Later, drive back to Delhi. Tour ends with sweet memories
