Delhi To Bharatpur Bird Sanctuary Tour
Duration : 05 Nights / 06 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Sultanpur - Agra - Fatehpur Sikri - Bharatpur

Day 01 : Delhi
Early morning arrival in Delhi. Upon arrival, transfer to the hotel for relaxation. In the afternoon, proceed for a sightseeing tour of Old and New Delhi visiting the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Jantar Mantar, India Gate, and other attractions. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 02 : Delhi - Sultanpur - Delhi
After breakfast, drive to Sultanpur Jheel, located about 50 km from Delhi. The sanctuary is rich in waterfowls and migratory birds including the common crane. Enjoy birdwatching before returning to Delhi. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 03 : Delhi - Agra - Fatehpur Sikri - Bharatpur
Drive to Agra in the morning and visit the world-famous Taj Mahal. Later, continue the journey to the deserted Mughal city of Fatehpur Sikri. Proceed further to Bharatpur and check in at the hotel near Keoladeo Ghana Bird Sanctuary. Overnight stay at hotel in Bharatpur.

Day 04 : Bharatpur
Enjoy a full day of bird watching walks in the Keoladeo Ghana Bird Sanctuary. Experience a boat ride through the marshes (subject to water levels). Overnight stay at hotel in Bharatpur.

Day 05 : Bharatpur
Another day dedicated to bird watching in the Keoladeo Ghana Bird Sanctuary, home to hundreds of resident and migratory bird species. Overnight stay at hotel in Bharatpur.

Day 06 : Bharatpur - Delhi
Morning visit to the park for final birdwatching. Later, drive back to Delhi. Upon arrival, transfer to the international airport to board the flight for onward journey. (Tour ends with sweet memories)

Delhi to Corbett Wildlife Tour Packages
Duration : 03 Nights / 04 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Corbett National Park - Delhi

Day 01 : Delhi - Corbett National Park
Morning drive to Corbett National Park. On arrival, check in at the wildlife resort. Nestled in the foothills of the Himalayas, on the banks of the Ramganga River in the Patli Dun Valley, it is India's oldest wildlife sanctuary named after Jim Corbett who spent many years in this region. Evening free at leisure. Overnight stay at resort.

Day 02 : Corbett National Park
Early morning nature walk inside the park. Return to the resort for breakfast. Later, visit Garjiya Temple and enjoy the scenic beauty of the Kosi River. After lunch, proceed for an afternoon jeep safari into the national park for wildlife viewing. Overnight stay at resort.

Day 03 : Corbett National Park
Enjoy both morning and afternoon jeep safaris in the national park for wildlife viewing. The park, famous for its tiger population, also shelters elephants, deer species, panthers, crocodiles, and an incredible variety of birdlife. Overnight stay at resort.

Day 04 : Corbett - Delhi
After breakfast at the resort, check out and drive back to Delhi. Upon arrival, transfer to the international airport to board your flight for onward journey. (Tour ends with sweet memories)

Delhi to Ranthambhore Wildlife Tour
Duration : 12 Nights / 13 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Jaipur - Ranthambore - Bharatpur - Fatehpur Sikri - Agra - Delhi - Corbett National Park - Delhi

Day 01 : Arrive in Delhi
On arrival at Delhi, meet our representative and transfer to the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 02 : Delhi Sightseeing
After breakfast, enjoy a full-day sightseeing tour of Delhi:

Old Delhi: Visit Red Fort, Jama Masjid (India's largest mosque), Raj Ghat (memorial of Mahatma Gandhi), and enjoy a rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk.
New Delhi: Visit Humayun's Tomb, Lotus Temple (Baha'i Temple), Qutub Minar, India Gate, and drive past Government buildings.
Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 03 : Delhi - Jaipur
After breakfast, drive to Jaipur. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Jaipur.

Day 04 : Jaipur Sightseeing
After breakfast, visit Amber Fort and enjoy an elephant ride. In the afternoon, explore the famous Pink City, known for its glorious past, vibrant culture, blue pottery, and inlaid brass work. Overnight stay at hotel in Jaipur.

Day 05 : Jaipur - Ranthambore
After breakfast, drive to Ranthambore. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at Ranthambore.

Day 06 : Ranthambore Safari
After breakfast, enjoy morning and afternoon jeep safaris in Ranthambore National Park, home to tigers and diverse wildlife. Overnight stay at Ranthambore.

Day 07 : Ranthambore - Bharatpur
After breakfast, drive to Bharatpur. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Bharatpur.

Day 08 : Bharatpur Sightseeing
After breakfast, visit the world-famous Keoladeo Ghana Bird Sanctuary. Later, explore the historic Fatehpur Sikri. Overnight stay at hotel in Bharatpur.

Day 09 : Bharatpur - Agra
After breakfast, drive to Agra. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Later, visit the magnificent Taj Mahal and other monuments of Agra. Overnight stay at hotel in Agra.

Day 10 : Agra - Delhi
After breakfast, drive back to Delhi. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 11 : Delhi - Corbett National Park
After breakfast, drive to Corbett National Park. On arrival, check in at the resort. Overnight stay at Corbett.

Day 12 : Corbett National Park
After breakfast, enjoy morning and afternoon jeep safaris in the park. Corbett is best known for its tigers but also supports elephants, deer, panthers, crocodiles, and rich birdlife. Overnight stay at resort in Corbett.

Day 13 : Corbett - Delhi
After breakfast, check out and drive back to Delhi. Later, transfer to the international airport to board your flight for onward journey. (Tour ends with sweet memories)

Delhi To Sariska Wildlife Tour Packages
Duration : 06 Nights / 07 Days
Destinations Covered : Delhi - Sariska - Ranthambore - Bharatpur - Delhi

Day 01 : Arrive Delhi
On arrival at Delhi International Airport, meet and assist by our representative. Transfer to your pre-booked hotel by private A/C car. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 02 : Delhi Sightseeing
After breakfast, enjoy a full-day sightseeing tour of Delhi visiting:

Jama Masjid - India's largest mosque
Red Fort - a UNESCO World Heritage Site
Raj Ghat - memorial of Mahatma Gandhi
India Gate - war memorial
Humayun's Tomb - precursor to the Taj Mahal
Lotus Temple - Baha'i House of Worship
Qutub Minar - world's tallest brick minaret
Akshardham Temple - modern marvel of architecture
Birla Mandir - also known as Laxmi Narayan Temple
Drive past Rashtrapati Bhavan (President's House) and Parliament House. Overnight stay at hotel in Delhi.

Day 03 : Delhi - Sariska
After breakfast, drive to Sariska. On arrival, check in at the hotel. In the evening, enjoy a visit to Sariska National Park. Overnight stay at hotel in Sariska.

Day 04 : Sariska - Ranthambore
Early morning visit to Sariska National Park for wildlife viewing. Later, drive to Ranthambore. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Overnight stay at hotel in Ranthambore.

Day 05 : Ranthambore National Park
After breakfast, enjoy a morning safari at Ranthambore National Park. Afternoon free at leisure. In the evening, embark on another visit to the park for wildlife spotting. Overnight stay at hotel in Ranthambore.

Day 06 : Ranthambore - Bharatpur
After breakfast, drive to Bharatpur. On arrival, check in at the hotel. In the evening, enjoy birdwatching at the Keoladeo Ghana National Park by cycle rickshaw or boat cruise (subject to water levels). Overnight stay at hotel in Bharatpur.

Day 07 : Bharatpur - Delhi
Early morning birdwatching tour at Bharatpur by cycle rickshaw or boat ride. Later, drive back to Delhi. On arrival, transfer to the airport to board your flight for onward journey. (Tour ends with sweet memories)
