=====20-Day Rajasthan Heritage Tour Itinerary=====
19 nights 20 days
Day 01: Arrival in Delhi
Meet our representative at the airport/railway station.
Transfer to hotel and relax.
Overnight stay in Delhi.
Day 02: Delhi - Mathura - Vrindavan - Agra
Visit the sacred temples of Mathura and Vrindavan.
Drive to Agra, check in at hotel.
Overnight stay in Agra.
Day 03: Agra Sightseeing
Visit the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Itmad-ud-Daulah Tomb.
Overnight stay in Agra.
Day 04: Agra - Fatehpur Sikri - Jaipur (235 km)
En route visit Fatehpur Sikri and Buland Darwaza.
Arrive in Jaipur, check in at hotel.
Overnight stay in Jaipur.
Day 05: Jaipur Sightseeing
Explore Amber Fort with an optional elephant ride.
Visit City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal and Albert Hall.
Overnight stay in Jaipur.
Day 06: Jaipur - Pushkar
Drive to Pushkar and visit the famous Brahma Temple and Pushkar Lake.
Overnight stay in Pushkar.
Day 07: Pushkar - Ranthambhore
Drive to Ranthambhore and enjoy evening jungle safari.
Overnight stay in Ranthambhore.
Day 08: Ranthambhore - Kota
Early morning safari in Ranthambhore National Park.
Proceed to Kota, visit City Palace, Jagmandir and gardens.
Overnight stay in Kota.
Day 09: Kota - Bundi - Bijaipur
Sightseeing at Bundi Fort and Raniji Ki Baori.
Proceed to Bijaipur and stay at the heritage castle.
Overnight stay in Bijaipur.
Day 10: Bijaipur - Chittorgarh - Udaipur
Visit Chittorgarh Fort.
Drive to Udaipur, check in at hotel.
Overnight stay in Udaipur.
Day 11: Udaipur Sightseeing
Visit Jagdish Temple, City Palace Museum and Saheliyon Ki Bari.
Evening leisure by Lake Pichola.
Overnight stay in Udaipur.
Day 12: Udaipur - Kumbhalgarh
Visit Nagda & Eklingji Temples.
Explore Kumbhalgarh Fort and enjoy horse safari.
Overnight stay in Kumbhalgarh.
Day 13: Kumbhalgarh - Jodhpur
Drive to Jodhpur and check in at hotel.
Overnight stay in Jodhpur.
Day 14: Jodhpur Sightseeing
Visit Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada and Umaid Bhawan Palace.
Jeep safari to Bishnoi Village for cultural experiences.
Overnight stay in Jodhpur.
Day 15: Jodhpur - Osian - Jaisalmer
En route visit Jain temples at Osian.
Check in at hotel in Jaisalmer.
Overnight stay in Jaisalmer.
Day 16: Jaisalmer Sightseeing
Visit Patwon Ki Haveli, Salim Singh Ki Haveli, Sonar Fort and Gadisar Lake.
Enjoy camel safari and sunset in the Thar Desert.
Overnight stay in Jaisalmer.
Day 17: Jaisalmer - Bikaner
Visit Gajner Palace en route.
Explore Junagarh Fort, Bikaner and Lalgarh Palace.
Overnight stay in Bikaner.
Day 18: Bikaner - Mandawa
Drive to Mandawa in the Shekhawati region.
Visit Mukandgarh Fort, Nawalgarh Fort, and beautifully painted havelis.
Overnight stay in Mandawa.
Day 19: Mandawa - Delhi
Drive back to Delhi.
Free time for personal activities.
Overnight stay in Delhi.
Day 20: Delhi Sightseeing & Departure
Visit Birla Temple, India Gate, PM House, Humayun's Tomb, Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Gandhi Memorial and Jama Masjid.
Transfer to airport/railway station.
Tour ends with royal memories of Rajasthan.
Rajasthan Heritage Tour Inclusions
Private car with English-speaking driver
Comfortable accommodation at heritage hotels
Breakfast included (other meals as per package)
Guided sightseeing & entrance fees at major attractions
Desert camel safari & cultural activities
Jungle safari at Ranthambhore National Park
